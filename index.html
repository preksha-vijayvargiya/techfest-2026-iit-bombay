Accommodation FAQs

What documents are required during check-in? +
You must present your Original College Student ID card along with a valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card / Passport / Driving License) and your Techfest Accommodation Booking Confirmation QR.
What are the check-in and check-out timings? +
Check-in opens at 06:00 AM on 18th December 2026. Standard daily check-out time is 10:00 PM on your final booked day. 24/7 helpdesk is available at Main Gate entrance.
Are food coupons included in the stay? +
Yes! Every accommodation pass includes complimentary daily breakfast and dinner coupons redeemable at the IIT Bombay Student Hostels Mess.
